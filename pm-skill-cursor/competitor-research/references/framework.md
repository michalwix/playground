# Competitor Analysis Framework

## Analysis Types

### 1. Pricing Analysis
Compare pricing models, tiers, and strategies.

**What to capture:**
- Plan names and positioning
- Price points (monthly vs annual)
- What's included in each tier
- Free tier / freemium approach
- Trial length and terms
- Discount strategies (annual, first-time, etc.)
- Hidden costs / add-ons

**Template:**
| Tier | [Competitor] | Wix | Notes |
|------|--------------|-----|-------|
| Free | ... | ... | ... |
| Basic | $X/mo | $Y/mo | ... |
| Pro | $X/mo | $Y/mo | ... |
| Business | $X/mo | $Y/mo | ... |

### 2. Feature Matrix
Side-by-side feature comparison.

**Categories to include:**
- Core features (table stakes)
- Advanced features
- Integrations
- Support options
- Limits and quotas

**Template:**
| Feature | [Competitor] | Wix | Winner |
|---------|--------------|-----|--------|
| Feature 1 | ✅ | ✅ | Tie |
| Feature 2 | ✅ | ❌ | Them |
| Feature 3 | ❌ | ✅ | Us |
| Feature 4 | ✅ (limited) | ✅ (full) | Us |

### 3. Flow Analysis
Compare user journeys and experiences.

**Flows to analyze:**
- Signup / onboarding
- Plan selection
- Checkout / payment
- Upgrade experience
- Downgrade experience
- Cancellation / retention

**What to capture:**
- Number of steps
- Friction points
- Upsell moments
- Retention tactics
- Mobile experience

### 4. SWOT Analysis
Comprehensive strategic analysis.

**Strengths (Internal positive)**
What do they do well? Why do customers choose them?

**Weaknesses (Internal negative)**
Where do they fall short? Common complaints?

**Opportunities (External positive)**
Market gaps they're not addressing? Trends they're missing?

**Threats (External negative)**
Where are they ahead of us? What should worry us?

## Research Sources

### Primary Sources (Most reliable)
- Competitor's website and pricing page
- Their public documentation
- Their blog and announcements
- App stores (reviews, descriptions)

### Secondary Sources
- G2, Capterra, TrustRadius reviews
- Reddit discussions
- Twitter/social mentions
- News articles
- Industry reports

### Competitor Intelligence Tools
- SimilarWeb (traffic, engagement)
- BuiltWith (technology stack)
- Owler (company info)
- Crunchbase (funding, growth)

## Analysis Do's and Don'ts

### DO:
- Use recent data (pricing changes frequently)
- Note the date of your research
- Include sources/links
- Consider different customer segments
- Look at reviews for real user perspective
- Consider their trajectory (improving or declining?)

### DON'T:
- Rely on outdated information
- Cherry-pick only favorable comparisons
- Ignore their strengths
- Assume their public info is complete
- Forget about indirect competitors
