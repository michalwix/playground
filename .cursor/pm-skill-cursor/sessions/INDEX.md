---

## How to Use

- Sessions are created automatically when PM starts research
- Each session gets its own directory: `YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>/`
- This index is updated after each session

## Session Statuses

- `in-progress` - Research ongoing
- `completed` - Research finished with findings
- `paused` - Research paused, can resume later
